# World News — Wednesday, 25 November 2026

A front-page-style image summing up the world news of Wednesday
25 November 2026, in six stories. The image is `entry.png`
(1024 × 1024 PNG); `main.ts` copies it to the output directory.

## The six stories

All six were reported on 25 November 2026 (in Asian morning editions) or
happened that day; each was verified against wire reporting before the
image was drawn.

1. **Putin warns retaliation "inevitable"** — Russia's president said
   retaliation was inevitable as Ukraine keeps striking Russian oil
   refineries, as the war's campaign against energy targets widened.
   Reported 25 November 2026 (WireMinute wire round-up).
2. **175 migrants missing off Senegal** — About 175 people are
   unaccounted for after contact was lost with a Europe-bound wooden
   boat (pirogue) that left Dakar around 9–10 November; local officials
   and the NGO Action for Human Rights and Friendship put the missing
   at about 172–175. Reported by Reuters, 24–25 November 2026.
3. **Congo rebels pull back from Bunia** — The Convention for the
   Popular Revolution (CRP), led by Thomas Lubanga, halted its advance
   on Bunia, capital of Ituri province in DR Congo, and withdrew from
   areas seized after days of fighting, citing Uganda-mediated talks.
   Announced 24 November, reported worldwide 25 November 2026
   (Al Jazeera).
4. **Baghdad seizes Kurdish oilfields** — Iraqi Prime Minister Ali
   al-Zaidi ordered the federal Oil Ministry to take over oilfields in
   Diyala, Kirkuk, Salahuddin and Nineveh provinces under Kurdish
   security control, and the border authority to take over all
   crossings in the Kurdistan region, after a smuggling probe at the
   state North Oil Company. Kurdish leaders rejected the order.
   Ordered 24 November, reported worldwide 25 November 2026 (Reuters,
   Iraq Oil Report).
5. **Xiamen orders 50 Boeing jets — $7.4bn** — China Southern said its
   subsidiary Xiamen Airlines agreed to buy 50 Boeing 737-8 aircraft
   at a list price of about $7.4 billion, subject to regulatory
   approval. Agreed 24 November, reported 25 November 2026 (Reuters).
6. **Houthi drone hits Riyadh tower — 11 hurt** — Yemen's Houthi
   fighters struck the tallest tower in Riyadh, the 385-metre Public
   Investment Fund headquarters, for the second time in less than a
   week, wounding at least 11 people. Britain imposed sanctions on the
   Houthis and four officials the same day. 24 November, carried in
   25 November editions (Reuters, BBC).

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This writes `out/entry.png`, byte-identical to `entry.png` in this
repository. Node 22, standard library only; no network is used.
